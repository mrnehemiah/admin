# HTML Modules

Kila module ina HTML yake. JavaScript na CSS za pamoja zinabaki kwenye `../js/` na `../css/`.

- `dashboard.html` — Dashboard: Muhtasari wa marketplace.
- `users.html` — Watumiaji: Usimamizi wa akaunti na majukumu.
- `sellers.html` — Sellers & Stores: Usimamizi wa wauzaji na maduka.
- `seller-applications.html` — Maombi ya Seller: Mapitio ya maombi ya wauzaji.
- `verification.html` — Verification: Uthibitishaji wa watumiaji na maduka.
- `products.html` — Bidhaa: Usimamizi wa bidhaa.
- `categories.html` — Categories: Usimamizi wa categories na subcategories.
- `orders.html` — Orders: Usimamizi wa maagizo.
- `payments.html` — Payments: Malipo na miamala.
- `wallets.html` — Wallet & Ledger: Wallet na kumbukumbu za ledger.
- `withdrawals.html` — Withdrawals: Maombi ya kutoa fedha.
- `commissions.html` — Commissions: Sheria na viwango vya commission.
- `delivery.html` — Delivery: Usimamizi wa usafirishaji.
- `disputes.html` — Disputes: Migogoro na malalamiko.
- `support.html` — Support Tickets: Tiketi za msaada.
- `promotions.html` — Promotions: Coupons na promotions.
- `announcements.html` — Announcements: Matangazo ya marketplace.
- `notifications.html` — Notifications: Taarifa za admin.
- `reports.html` — Reports & Analytics: Ripoti na takwimu.
- `settings.html` — Marketplace Settings: Mipangilio ya marketplace.
- `security.html` — Security Center: Usalama wa mfumo.
- `audit.html` — Audit Logs: Historia ya matendo ya admin.
- `admin-account.html` — Admin Account: Akaunti ya Admin Mkuu.
- `ai.html` — ZUNE AI Control Center: Usimamizi wa huduma za AI.
- `health.html` — System Health: Afya ya mfumo.
- `database.html` — Database Monitoring: Ufuatiliaji wa database.
- `backup.html` — Backup & Recovery: Backup na urejeshaji wa data.
